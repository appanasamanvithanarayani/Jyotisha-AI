## Edition, rights, and evidence method

The limited comparison source is Balabhadra Daivajña, *The Jewel of Annual Astrology: A Parallel Sanskrit-English Critical Edition of Balabhadra’s Hāyanaratna*, edited, translated and annotated by Martin Gansten (Leiden/Boston: Brill, 2020), eISBN 978-90-04-43371-7. Its [publisher front matter](https://brill.com/display/book/9789004433717/9789004433717_webready_content_text.pdf) and [JSTOR catalog](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.1163/j.ctv2gjwzsx) identify CC BY 4.0 for **original material**, while the publisher expressly excludes referenced third-party material from blanket coverage. The checks below rely principally on Gansten's original explanatory prose and notes. Where chapter text is discussed, it is identified as the edition's rendering of Balabhadra, not as Gansten's independent doctrinal assertion. No third-party quotation is reproduced here. CC BY coverage is adequate for consulting and paraphrasing Gansten's original explanatory material in this attributed review; it is **not** a blanket project clearance for source-text corpus extraction, the Sanskrit witnesses, third-party excerpts, or model-weight distribution. The two blocked local BPHS PDFs were not used and retain their manifest permissions.

**Access limitation:** JSTOR exposes the edition, chapter ranges and short chapter previews; Brill's indexed original pages expose the relevant passages, but direct full-page opening returned HTTP 403 during this pass. The printed-page locators below were cross-checked against the edition's [index](https://brill.com/display/book/9789004433717/back-4.xml) where possible. These are **source-text search/index checks**, not a line-by-line inspection of complete page images or Sanskrit manuscript witnesses. No qualified Sanskrit/Jyotisha reviewer has signed off. “Partly supports” below means only the specified subclaim is evidenced, not that the whole publisher answer is true or verified. *Hāyanaratna* concerns Tājika annual astrology; it cannot automatically certify general natal or Parāśarī claims.
