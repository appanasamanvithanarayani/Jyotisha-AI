## Rights layers and jurisdictions

1. **Base Sanskrit work:** Its ancient/traditional attribution strongly
   suggests a different rights position from 19th-century additions, but
   authorship, this edition's wording, and any later editorial additions must
   be separated. Ancient status alone does not clear the whole volume.
2. **Commentary and editing:** These may have independent protection. In
   India, [Copyright Act §22](https://copyright.gov.in/Copyright_Act_1957/chapter_v.html)
   generally runs a literary work's term through 60 years from the beginning
   of the calendar year after the author's death; special cases differ.
   Publication in 1899 therefore does not by itself establish expiry for
   identified authors. Originality and precise editorial scope also need
   assessment.
3. **Digitization:** A purely mechanical scan may add no new US copyright
   authorship under the [US Copyright Office's mere-copy guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/docs/compendium-122214.pdf),
   but the scanner, source institution, possible enhancements, and applicable
   access/contract terms are unidentified. The Commons description does not
   establish authority to digitize or the terms of the source collection.
   India and any other relevant jurisdiction require separate assessment.
4. **Wikisource transcription:** It is user-edited and unproofread; its
   [page-level CC BY-SA notice](https://sa.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%A4%AA%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A0%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D:%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AC%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D_(%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%AC%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%9F%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D).pdf/%E0%A5%A7)
   is not a license for the scanned book and not an independently cleared
   training transcript.

The US position is **more favorable but conditional**: for an authentic
1899 publication, the ordinary maximum term for pre-1978 published works
has elapsed by 2026; foreign-work restoration under
[17 USC §104A](https://copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) does not grant a
term beyond the otherwise applicable US term. The [US Copyright Office's
duration guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) expressly
addresses US-published pre-1931 works, so it should not be substituted for
an edition-specific foreign-work analysis. Confirm first-publication date,
identity of this scan, and absence of later protected material.

Commons' India/US public-domain labels are **site claims**, not a grant of
permission or a legal opinion. [Commons reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia)
says reusers must verify copyright information and that Wikimedia provides
no warranty; public-domain status can differ by country. Assess at least
India (likely source and project jurisdiction) and the US (Commons hosting
and possible distribution), then each other place of training, storage, or
dataset/weights distribution. Those locations have not been specified.

## Confidence and minimum clearance evidence

**Confidence:** high that the Commons/Wikisource pages *claim* a 1899
Jñānasāgara printing and India/US public-domain status; moderate that the
unproofread first-page transcription identifies the contributors above; low
that the entire edition and scan are cleared for this project's intended
uses. No independent legal clearance is asserted.

Minimum next evidence: (1) independently cataloged edition record and
verified title/colophon data for the *specific* scan; (2) identities, death
dates, and contribution scope of commentator and both editors, or another
documented India public-domain basis; (3) scan-origin and digitization/access
terms from the holding or scanning institution; and (4) a rights-qualified
India/US opinion, extended to any other actual training and distribution
jurisdictions, expressly covering commercial/noncommercial training,
source-derived datasets, and weights. If any right remains live, obtain an
edition-specific written grant from the identified holder. Until then, do
not add this candidate as a training-approved source or ingest it.
