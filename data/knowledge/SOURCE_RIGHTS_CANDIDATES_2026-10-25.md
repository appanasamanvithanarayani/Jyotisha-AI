## Recommendation and approval evidence

**Prioritize candidate 1, the Brill/Gansten 2020 edition, for a narrowly scoped, rights-documented source pilot.** It uniquely combines a specific scholarly Sanskrit-English edition, publisher-origin digital copy, and an explicit CC BY 4.0 notice. The license is stronger evidence than a PDM label. Before approval, a rights-qualified reviewer should record: (a) the publisher front matter/ISBN and the CC BY 4.0 legal text; (b) which pages/passages are original licensed text/translation versus referenced third-party excerpts or images, excluding the latter unless separately cleared; (c) the actual training, storage, dataset and weights-distribution jurisdictions and an attribution/modification plan; and (d) whether the intended model/dataset release poses any residual rights question needing counsel. A substantive reviewer must separately confirm which of the 20 Q&A claims the **Tājika** source can actually bear. Approval of the source would **not** verify a Q&A row or authorize importing the 1899/Santhanam editions.

**Exact next action:** send the scoped clarification below to [Brill Rights & Permissions](https://brill.com/page/resources_rights) at `rights@degruyterbrill.com`, attach/link the book's front-matter notice, and obtain a written answer about the CC BY boundary and any non-CC passages. The CC BY-covered original material does not require a new grant merely because it may be used for AI training; the inquiry addresses the front matter's explicit exclusion and intended downstream uses. If they cannot clear an excerpt, omit that excerpt or seek its actual holder's permission. In parallel, a rights-qualified reviewer can document the title-specific clearance memo; **do not change the source manifest until that memo exists**.

> Subject: Rights scope for the CC BY 4.0 edition of *The Jewel of Annual Astrology* (eISBN 9789004433717)
>
> We are documenting rights for a Jyotisha research and AI-training project, including commercial/noncommercial text analysis and model training, source-grounded educational Q&A, possible publication of short derived examples, and distribution of trained model weights. Your 2020 edition credits Martin Gansten and states that CC BY 4.0 applies to original material but not necessarily to referenced third-party text or images. Could you confirm whether Gansten's English translation, Sanskrit critical text, annotations, and the digital edition are covered by that CC BY notice? Please identify any passages, quotations, images or other components excluded from it and the appropriate rightsholders/permission path. We will provide attribution and record modifications, and will not assume the license covers excluded material. Please also flag any publisher-imposed access terms relevant to corpus retention or the uses described above. We request clarification or a separate grant only for material **outside** the CC BY scope; no source text is attached.

This request was **drafted, not sent**. It is not evidence of permission. No other source or data was downloaded, copied, ingested or altered during this investigation.
