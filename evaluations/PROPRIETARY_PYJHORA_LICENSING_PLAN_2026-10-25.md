**Decision:** Keep Project Jyotisha AI proprietary. PyJHora, `pyswisseph`, and Swiss Ephemeris remain **evaluation-only**; no main-runtime integration, redistribution, or public chart service is approved by this report. This is a technical/rights inventory, not legal advice or a license grant. Have qualified counsel review the intended jurisdictions and signed terms before release.

## Current project and rights layers

The project's `pyproject.toml` declares `license = "Proprietary"`, Python >=3.11, and only `pypdf==5.9.0` plus `astronomy-engine==2.1.19` as runtime dependencies. No PyJHora adapter or MCP server is in the app. A separate synthetic [standalone probe](PYJHORA_STANDALONE_PROBE_2026-10-25.md) ran PyJHora 4.8.7 with `pyswisseph` 2.10.3.2 in an ignored environment, without `.se1` files; a diagnostic showed Moshier fallback. It does not validate output or grant distribution rights.

## Staged technical integration, gated

1. **Rights gate — now blocked:** Obtain and archive written, version-specific PyJHora and binding grants plus the signed/paid Swiss Professional contract, or document legally reviewed substitutes. Record exact grant scope, contributors, data files, deployment modes, attribution, renewal, and commercial use. Do not change `pyproject.toml` or app imports before this gate.
2. **Validation gate:** In an isolated environment, acquire the authorized `.se1` set, record SHA-256 and source, assert returned Swiss flags rather than silently accepting Moshier fallback, and compare multiple synthetic planetary, Lagna, Panchanga, and dasha cases against independent reference outputs under matched ayanamsha, zodiac, house, offset, and date conventions. Existing cross-engine agreement is only a smoke check. No personal birth details.
3. **Implementation gate:** Only after rights and validation, pin compatible Python/`PyJHora`/binding/data versions and hashes; add a small direct-import calculation adapter behind an explicit backend setting. Keep the current `astronomy-engine` path intact as a fallback, preserve structured provenance and error reporting, and avoid the unlicensed MCP wrapper. Test invalid dates, timezone offsets, ephemeris absence/fallback, and process/thread behavior. A direct dependency means its license must be compatible with the app's distribution plan.
4. **Release gate:** Counsel approves the combined deployment and notices; CI checks licenses, hashes, ephemeris availability, and synthetic/reference cases; deployment policy handles birth-data privacy, network isolation, and Swiss renewal/API-customer obligations. Never label chart output verified solely because PyJHora returned it.

**Smallest next action:** Send the three drafted rights questions, starting with the PyJHora maintainer, and retain the written responses. If PyJHora cannot grant proprietary terms for all relevant contributions, do not buy a Swiss license on the assumption that it solves the whole stack; consider a differently licensed deterministic engine instead.
