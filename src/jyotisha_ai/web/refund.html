Cancellation

You can cancel before payment by closing the payment window. No charge should be made. Because a successful purchase is delivered immediately, it cannot normally be cancelled after the digital feature has been delivered or used.

When a refund may be requested

You may request a refund when:

  • you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
  • the payment was successful but the purchased feature was not unlocked or delivered;
  • the amount charged was different from the amount shown at checkout; or
  • you believe the payment was unauthorised.

A change of mind after a digital feature has been delivered or used is normally not eligible for a refund. This does not limit any rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

How to request a refund

Processing timeline

We will review a complete request within 5 business days. If approved, we will initiate the refund to the original payment method within 7 business days. After initiation, the bank or payment provider may take an additional 5–10 business days to show the credit. Duplicate or unauthorised-payment claims may require additional verification.

Failed or pending payments

If a payment fails but an amount is debited, the bank or payment provider will normally reverse it automatically. If it is not reversed within the timeline shown by your bank, contact us with the payment details so we can check its status.
