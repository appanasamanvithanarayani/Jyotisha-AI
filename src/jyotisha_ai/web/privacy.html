What this app does

Jyotirmaya AstroChat shows a daily Panchang, a festival calendar, a Kundli (birth chart), a sign-by-sign Rashiphal, a Moon sign profile, tarot readings, palm reading and a chat about your chart. Calculations are done from astronomy; they follow traditional rules.

What information is used

  • Birth details (date, place and time of birth) are sent to our server to calculate your chart. We do not store the exact date, place or time or write them to logs. After a successful Kundli, we retain only the birth year and a broad age range with the anonymous usage record described below. There are no accounts.
  • Your language, city and the birth details of your last chart are saved only on your own device, in the browser's local storage, so you do not have to type them again. They never go to our server for storage. Use "Forget my saved birth details" on the Kundli page, or clear the app's data, to remove them.
  • Location is read only when you tap a location button, and only with your permission. It is used to suggest a nearby city and is not stored.
  • Chat questions. When the chat assistant is switched on, the question you type and the calculated labels of your chart (signs, houses, periods; no name, no birth place) are sent to an AI service (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) to write the reply. Do not type personal details, health records or anything you want kept private into the chat.
  • Your name and date of birth (if you enter them when you first open the app) are saved only on your device to greet you and suggest your Moon sign. They are not stored on our server.
  • Palm photos. When you use palm reading, the photo you take is made smaller on your phone and sent to our server, which passes it to an AI service (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) to describe the lines of your palm. The photo is used only for that one reading: our server does not save it, write it to logs or link it to you. Take a photo of your palm only, without other people or documents in it.
  • Tarot readings. The cards are drawn on your phone; the card names and the topic you chose are sent to the AI service to write the reading. No personal details are sent.
  • App ratings and feedback. If you choose 1 or 2 stars, the reason you type, your selected app language, the rating and the time are stored privately for the developer to review in the admin panel. We do not ask for your name or birth details with feedback. A 5-star choice opens the Google Play listing so you can decide whether to post a public review there.
  • Payments. Razorpay Checkout collects payment details; they do not reach our server. To create and verify a purchase, our server receives the Razorpay order ID, payment ID and signature. For reconciliation and anonymous admin totals, we retain only a one-way hash of a successful payment ID, its date, purchased feature and amount. This prevents the same callback being counted twice without storing card, bank or UPI credentials.
  • Anonymous usage measurement. We set a random first-party identifier so we can count new and returning users, anonymously count app installations, understand which features are opened or successfully produce a result (for example, Kundli, Tarot or palm reading), and count aggregate ad events such as requested, shown, rewarded, dismissed and failed. We store the anonymous identifier, first and last activity dates, active-day count, feature totals, app platform and first reported installation date, and—after a successful Kundli—birth year and a broad age range. We do not store ad content, ad revenue or an advertising identifier with this usage record. We also do not store your name, IP address, exact birth date, birth place or time, questions, photos or generated answers.
  • Android app advertising measurement. The Android app uses Meta App Events to measure app installs and launches from advertising campaigns. Meta may receive the app identifier, device and advertising identifiers, IP address, and basic app/device information for attribution and aggregated ad reporting. We do not send your birth details, palm photo or chat questions to Meta through this integration. You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier in Android settings.

AI-generated answers

Some chat replies are written by an AI service. They can be wrong or unsuitable. Tap "Report this answer" under a reply to tell us; it opens your email app with the question and answer filled in so you can review and send it. We use reports only to improve the service.

Not advice

Astrology is traditional belief and is offered for reflection and entertainment. Nothing here is medical, legal, financial or professional advice, and nothing is guaranteed. Calendar and festival dates follow simple conventional rules and can differ by a day from a printed Panchang or a regional custom. Please verify important dates with a trusted source.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Data sources

Place names and coordinates come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Planet positions are calculated with the open-source Astronomy Engine library.
