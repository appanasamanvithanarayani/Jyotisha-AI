What this app does

Jyotisha AI shows a daily Panchang, a festival calendar, a Kundli (birth chart), a sign-by-sign Rashiphal and a chat about your chart. Calculations are done from astronomy; they follow traditional rules.

What information is used

  • Birth details (date, place and time of birth) are sent to our server only to calculate your chart. Our server does not store them, write them to logs or link them to you. There are no accounts.
  • Your language, city and the birth details of your last chart are saved only on your own device, in the browser's local storage, so you do not have to type them again. They never go to our server for storage. Use "Forget my saved birth details" on the Kundli page, or clear the app's data, to remove them.
  • Location is read only when you tap a location button, and only with your permission. It is used to suggest a nearby city and is not stored.
  • Chat questions. When the chat assistant is switched on, the question you type and the calculated labels of your chart (signs, houses, periods; no name, no birth place) are sent to an AI service (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) to write the reply. Do not type personal details, health records or anything you want kept private into the chat.

AI-generated answers

Some chat replies are written by an AI service. They can be wrong or unsuitable. Tap "Report this answer" under a reply to tell us; it opens your email app with the question and answer filled in so you can review and send it. We use reports only to improve the service.

Not advice

Astrology is traditional belief and is offered for reflection and entertainment. Nothing here is medical, legal, financial or professional advice, and nothing is guaranteed. Calendar and festival dates follow simple conventional rules and can differ by a day from a printed Panchang or a regional custom. Please verify important dates with a trusted source.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Data sources

Place names and coordinates come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Planet positions are calculated with the open-source Astronomy Engine library.
